# Night City hobby asset index

These local files are used for a personal, non-commercial learning preview.

- Official visual assets are local copies from the CD PROJEKT RED press materials listed in `../official/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.
- The gallery exposes local PNG/WebP files with a download link so their provenance is visible. The page currently uses eight distinct WebP derivatives: desktop hero, vertical hero, Johnny, Night City vista, Night City day, Japantown, Black Sapphire, and street view.
- Story entries are original summaries and research links. Game text, shards, and long wiki passages are not copied into the site.
- Music entries are metadata and official listening links only. No Cyberpunk 2077 or Edgerunners audio is bundled here.
- Before publishing or redistributing this site, re-check each asset's current license and the CD PROJEKT RED Fan Content Guidelines.
