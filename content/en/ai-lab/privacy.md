## Page data

AI Lab search, track filters, sorting, and reading-theme preferences run in the browser. These features do not require an account or ask visitors to submit personal information.

## Contact

For questions about page data, use the email address on the contact page. The hosting provider may process ordinary access logs under its own policies; the exact scope depends on the active hosting platform.
