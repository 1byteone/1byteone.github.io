## 2. Contact form

The contact page uses `mailto:` to open the visitor's local mail client. This static Hugo site does not upload the form fields or write them to a database. Processing after sending is handled by the visitor's mail provider.

## 3. Browser preferences

The theme center may store the default theme, recent themes, and favorites in browser `localStorage`. Clearing site data removes these preferences. They are not sent to this site.

## 4. Static hosting logs

The deployment platform, CDN, or host may record access logs under its own terms, such as IP address, timestamp, request path, and browser information. This site does not add separate analytics or advertising trackers; platform logs remain the host's responsibility.

## 5. External links

GitHub, reference sites, and mail services have their own privacy policies. Their policies apply after leaving this site.

## 6. Scope

This page describes a personal learning project and is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal review. Contact the maintainer by email to request deletion of information sent by email.
