# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors include hiring managers, senior stakeholders, Saudi and Arabic-speaking readers, technical peers, and friends who may first arrive for a small utility rather than for the portfolio itself. They need a site that is easy to trust, quick to understand, and useful without an account.

## Product Purpose

termfolio is Waleed Alhamed's personal portfolio, notebook, lab, and practical tools surface. It keeps the terminal identity and playful technical character while making the first impression credible enough to share broadly. Success means visitors understand who Waleed is, can read or skim his thinking, can inspect experiments without mistaking them for formal case studies, and can return for no-login tools.

## Brand Personality

Terminal-native, sharp, useful. The voice should feel direct and human, with Arabic written naturally in a polished Thmanyah-style mix of clear modern Arabic and light everyday phrasing. The site can be playful, but it should never feel unserious or careless.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic executive portfolio templates, stock corporate landing pages, over-polished SaaS marketing, childish hacker cosplay, bloated case-study language, literal Arabic translation, and visual changes that bury the existing terminal vibe.

## Design Principles

- Keep the terminal identity, but make trust signals visible.
- Make every playful feature useful on its own.
- Write Arabic as original interface copy, not translation.
- Separate experiments and play from professional claims.
- Prefer small, sharp interactions over heavy explanations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support bilingual English and Arabic experiences, including RTL layout and Thmanyah Arabic typography. Preserve readable contrast on dark themes, keyboard focus states, reduced-motion safety, clear labels, and mobile layouts that do not clip or overlap text.
